## Full description — max 4000
```
Leave helps soldiers plan unit leave together and keeps the number of people out on any given day within the unit's cap.

Stop guessing whether you can take that day off. Every member's leave lands on one shared calendar, and any day that goes over the cap is highlighted in red automatically.

WHO IT'S FOR
- Soldiers tired of asking around to avoid overlapping leave
- Anyone who wants a clear view of who's out and when
- People who like to keep track of promotion and discharge dates

KEY FEATURES
- Unit leave calendar — your unit's leave shows up like events on a shared calendar.
- Over-cap highlighting — if the number of people out exceeds the unit's maximum ratio (e.g. one third of everyone), that day turns red.
- Over-cap alerts — when your new leave pushes a day over the cap, everyone on leave that day gets a push notification.
- Fast leave entry — just a title, start date, and end date. Reason is optional.
- Create & find units — search and join a unit, or create one and set its maximum leave ratio.
- Automatic rank promotion — your current rank is calculated from your enlistment date and branch. See your next promotion and days until discharge.
- Profile — name, branch, rank, and photo so your unit can recognize you.

PRIVACY
For service operation and security, Leave collects access logs and this app's push notification records, with your consent at sign-up. You can view your own records anytime in the app, and data is encrypted in transit.

With Leave, planning time off is a plan — not a guessing game.
```
